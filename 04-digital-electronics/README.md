# Digital Electronics

## 📌 Overview

During my internship at Monk9 Tech, I studied the fundamentals of digital electronics and their role in modern electronic and VLSI systems.

The training helped me understand how digital logic forms the foundation of processors, controllers, integrated circuits and embedded systems.

## 📚 Topics Covered

- Digital and binary systems
- Logic gates
- Boolean algebra
- Combinational logic circuits
- Sequential logic circuits
- Flip-flops
- Registers
- Counters
- Multiplexers and Demultiplexers
- Digital circuit fundamentals
- Introduction to IC-based digital systems

## 🧠 Learning Experience

The concepts were explained through technical sessions and structured learning activities. The training helped me connect digital logic concepts with VLSI, embedded systems and real-world electronic applications.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome

I developed a stronger foundation in digital electronics and understood how digital logic concepts are used in the design of modern electronic systems and integrated circuits.

> The topics were studied as part of internship training and learning activities.
