# Semiconductor Fabrication

## 📌 Overview

During my internship at Monk9 Tech, I studied the semiconductor fabrication process and understood the major stages involved in converting silicon wafers into integrated circuits.

The concepts were learned through technical sessions, educational material and an industrial visit, where the overall semiconductor manufacturing process and related technologies were explained.

## 🏭 Fabrication Flow

The major stages covered include:

1. Silicon Purification
2. Crystal Growth
3. Wafer Slicing
4. Wafer Preparation
5. Oxidation
6. Photolithography
7. Etching
8. Doping / Ion Implantation
9. Thin Film Deposition
10. Chemical Mechanical Planarization (CMP)
11. Metallization
12. Wafer Testing
13. Dicing
14. Packaging
15. Final Testing

## 🔬 Key Concepts

- Silicon wafer manufacturing
- Photolithography
- Etching
- Doping
- Deposition
- CMP
- Metallization
- Wafer testing
- IC packaging

## 🏭 Industrial Visit

As part of the learning experience, I participated in an industrial visit where the semiconductor manufacturing environment and fabrication-related processes were explained.

The visit helped me connect the theoretical concepts studied during the internship with real-world semiconductor manufacturing practices.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome

This training helped me understand how semiconductor fabrication processes contribute to the manufacturing of modern integrated circuits and how fabrication technology forms the foundation of VLSI.

> Note: The fabrication processes were studied theoretically and through industry-oriented explanations; they were not personally performed as hands-on fabrication experiments.
