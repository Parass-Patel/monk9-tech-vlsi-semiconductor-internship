# VLSI & Physical Design

## 📌 Overview

During my internship at Monk9 Tech, I studied the fundamentals of VLSI and the physical design flow used in integrated circuit development.

The training helped me understand how a digital design progresses from RTL-level concepts towards physical implementation.

## 📚 Topics Covered

- VLSI fundamentals
- IC design flow
- RTL design overview
- Synthesis
- Floorplanning
- Placement
- Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS)
- Routing
- Physical verification
- Design constraints
- Area, power and timing concepts
- ASIC physical design flow

## 🔄 Physical Design Flow

A high-level physical design flow covered during the training was:

```text
RTL Design
    ↓
Logic Synthesis
    ↓
Floorplanning
    ↓
Power Planning
    ↓
Placement
    ↓
Clock Tree Synthesis
    ↓
Routing
    ↓
Physical Verification
    ↓
Sign-off
