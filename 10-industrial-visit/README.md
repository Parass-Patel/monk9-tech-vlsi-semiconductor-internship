# Industrial Visit — Semiconductor Manufacturing

## 📌 Overview

As part of my internship at Monk9 Tech, I participated in an industrial visit related to semiconductor technology and manufacturing.

The visit provided practical exposure to an industrial environment and helped me understand how semiconductor manufacturing concepts are applied in real-world facilities.

## 🏭 What I Observed & Learned

During the industrial visit, I gained an understanding of:

- Semiconductor manufacturing environment
- Wafer and semiconductor processing concepts
- Photolithography
- Etching
- Chemical Mechanical Planarization (CMP)
- Wafer processing and handling
- Packaging concepts
- Testing and quality considerations
- Cleanroom and manufacturing practices
- Semiconductor equipment and process flow

## 🧠 Learning Experience

The industrial visit helped me connect the theoretical concepts covered during the internship with real-world semiconductor manufacturing processes.

It gave me a better understanding of how different stages of semiconductor manufacturing contribute to the development of integrated circuits.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome

The visit strengthened my understanding of semiconductor technology and provided valuable insight into industrial manufacturing practices, process flow and quality considerations.

> The semiconductor manufacturing processes were observed and explained during the industrial visit; no claim of personally performing fabrication processes is made.
