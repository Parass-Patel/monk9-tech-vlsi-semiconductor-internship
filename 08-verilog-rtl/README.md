# Verilog & RTL Design

## 📌 Overview

During my internship at Monk9 Tech, I was introduced to Verilog and RTL (Register Transfer Level) design concepts used in digital and VLSI design.

The training helped me understand how digital hardware functionality can be described using hardware description languages and how RTL fits into the VLSI design flow.

## 📚 Topics Covered

- Verilog fundamentals
- Hardware Description Language (HDL)
- RTL design concepts
- Modules and ports
- Combinational logic
- Sequential logic
- Registers
- Counters
- Digital design modelling
- Simulation fundamentals
- RTL-to-gate-level design flow
- Introduction to synthesis

## 🧠 Learning Experience

Through technical sessions and learning activities, I developed a foundational understanding of Verilog and RTL design and their role in digital hardware development.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome

I gained basic knowledge of describing digital hardware using Verilog and understanding how RTL designs are used as an important stage in the VLSI design flow.

> The topics were studied as part of internship training and learning activities.
